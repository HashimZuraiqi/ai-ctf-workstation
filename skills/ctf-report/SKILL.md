---
name: ctf-report
description: Turn a solved authorized CTF challenge into a reproducible team writeup.
---

Use after or near completion.

Produce a concise reproducible writeup containing:

1. challenge goal and category
2. relevant files/services and protections
3. key observations/evidence
4. important hypotheses and how they were tested
5. exploit/solver design
6. exact reproduction commands
7. final validation method
8. useful lessons or pitfalls

Do not invent missing evidence. Do not publish flags/writeups before competition rules allow disclosure.
